The founder

World No. 1 at 43. He knows the long way up.

Rohan Bopanna spent two decades on tour before he held a Grand Slam men’s doubles trophy. RBTA is everything that journey taught him, written into a curriculum: get fit first, get the basics right, then learn to play smart.

The RBTA method

  • 01

    Fitness first

    Children get fit before they get technical: core strength, stamina and agility that serve any sport.

  • 02

    Right basics

    Sound technique from day one, built through off-court lessons, repetition and on-court practice.

  • 03

    Play smart

    Fitness, mental strength and skill put to work in real competition.

The pathway

The court grows with the player.

Seven stages, one continuous curriculum. Every player is assessed and placed where they will progress fastest, then moved up when they are ready, not when the calendar says so.

  1. 01

    Ages 4–6 · Building the basics

    Red Ball

    The first step. Smaller courts and bigger, slower balls so a four-year-old can rally on day one.

    • Coordination, balance and footwork
    • Racquet skills through play
    • Confidence in a group
    Enquire about Red Ball
  2. 02

    Ages 7–9 · Expanding skills

    Orange Ball

    A bigger court and a faster ball. Technique is refined and players learn to build a point.

    • Stroke mechanics and consistency
    • Rallying and first point play
    • Tactical awareness
    Enquire about Orange Ball
  3. 03

    Ages 9–12 · Ready for the full court

    Green Dot

    The last stage before the yellow ball. Power, control and real match play on a full-size court.

    • Power with control
    • Match-play strategy
    • Speed and endurance
    Enquire about Green Dot
  4. 04

    Ages 11+ · Full court, yellow ball

    Junior Development

    A solid technical and tactical base, with fitness training and a first tournament calendar.

    • Shot selection and point construction
    • Speed, agility and endurance
    • Local and regional tournaments
    Enquire about Junior Development
  5. 05

    Ranked juniors · National and ITF competition

    Junior Pro

    For promising juniors competing in national and international ranking events, preparing for the professional circuit.

    • High-intensity, specialised training
    • Tournament planning and travel guidance
    • Conditioning and injury prevention
    Enquire about Junior Pro
  6. 06

    Elite · Full-time athletes

    High Performance

    Led by Director of Tennis Balachandran Manikkath with High Performance Coach Dragan Bukumirovic, and mentored by Rohan Bopanna.

    • Advanced technical and tactical work
    • Peak physical conditioning
    • Mentoring from Rohan Bopanna
    Enquire about High Performance
  7. 07

    Tour · Training base for touring pros

    Professional

    A home base for professional players, with the full performance team and the Talent Support Program behind them.

    • Individual coaching blocks
    • Recovery, physio and nutrition
    • Financial backing for proven players
    Enquire about Professional

Rohan Bopanna Tennis Development Foundation

Bhoomi means earth.

It is also where champions come from. The Bhoomi Program finds gifted children from underprivileged families, many of whom have never seen a tennis court, and gives them a home, a school and a world-class coaching team.

35
children on full scholarship today
400+
children seen at trials in the North-East and Jammu & Kashmir
10
years of support per child, through to 12th standard
₹12L
funds one child for one year, everything included
  1. Step 1

    Find

    Open trials for children aged 8 to 12 in small towns and rural districts. Athletic ability matters; prior tennis does not.

  2. Step 2

    Move

    Selected children move to Bengaluru: hostel, ICSE schooling, daily coaching, nutrition and sports science, at no cost to the family.

  3. Step 3

    Rise

    Twice-yearly reviews, two home visits a year, and a route into High Performance for those who excel.
